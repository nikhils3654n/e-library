About E-LIBRARY

E-LIBRARY is a digital book management and reading system designed for educational institutions and libraries such as colleges, universities and schools. It allows an organization to manage its complete book collection in one place, while students can easily discover and access the books available to them.

Instead of depending only on physical shelves and registers, the organization keeps its collection online, and every registered student can reach that collection from any device at any time.

  • ✅ One place for the whole book collection
  • ✅ Separate logins for students, librarians and administrators
  • ✅ No paper registers, no lost records
  • ✅ Works on a computer, tablet or mobile phone

How E-LIBRARY Works

Three steps take a student from the login page to a book.

1

Login to your account

Enter your Email or User ID, your password, and choose whether you are a student, a librarian or an administrator.

2

Search the collection

Browse the subject categories or search by title and author to see which books your organization has added.

3

Open and read

Open the book you need and read it on screen. Nothing has to be carried home and nothing can be lost.

Who Can Use E-LIBRARY?

The system has three types of users, and each one sees a different set of options after login.

🎓 Student

  • Read available books online

👤 Librarian

  • Add, edit and remove books

Common Questions

Click a question to open the answer.

Who can create an account on E-LIBRARY?

Accounts are created for members of the organization only. A college gives accounts to its own students, librarians and administrators.

Do I have to install any software?

No. E-LIBRARY opens in a normal web browser on a computer, tablet or mobile phone.

Can two students read the same book at the same time?

Yes. A digital copy does not have to be returned before the next student can open it.

What happens if I forget my password?

Use the "Forgot Password?" link on the login page, or contact the librarian of your organization.
